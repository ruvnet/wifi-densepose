Thickness is measured co-reception: how many transmissions both boards captured, from the (tx_mac, rx_seq) pairing index. Note what that is and is not — it counts co-observation of a common transmitter (nearly always the AP), gated by each board's own frame rate. It is not a measure of coupling between the pair, so a thick line does not mean those two boards hear each other well.
